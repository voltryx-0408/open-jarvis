# Open Jarvis Privacy Notice

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Open Jarvis is an Android assistant that can use Accessibility to inspect visible app content and interact with app controls when you request automation.

## Information processed

- **Commands and prompts:** Text or voice-derived commands that you submit to Jarvis.
- **Visible screen content:** When automation reads the active screen, visible text may be included in the context used to process your command.
- **Task history and memory:** The app may store command and task-result information locally to provide task history and memory features.
- **AI provider settings:** The app stores the AI provider settings you enter. API keys are intended to be stored in Android encrypted preferences.

## AI providers and network transmission

If you configure an online AI provider, command text, relevant screen text, and task context may be sent to that provider to process your request. The provider's own privacy terms and data-handling practices apply to that transmission. Review the selected provider before entering sensitive information. Do not use Jarvis on passwords, payment screens, private messages, or other sensitive content.

If you configure a local model, behavior depends on that model and the features you use. Network features may still connect to services you explicitly configure.

## Accessibility and overlay permissions

Accessibility is a powerful permission. When enabled, Jarvis can inspect visible app content and perform supported actions such as tapping controls or entering text. Overlay permission allows the app to display floating controls. Both are optional Android special-access permissions and can be revoked in Android Settings. Do not enable them unless you understand the access they grant.

The app should show an in-app disclosure before asking you to enable Accessibility. This notice does not replace that disclosure or any consent required by Google Play.

## Data storage and security

Task history and settings may be stored on your device. No app can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device updated and do not share API keys. If you use a third-party AI provider, review its retention and deletion options.

## Children and sensitive information

Do not enter sensitive personal information into prompts. A parent or guardian should review use of the app if appropriate.
